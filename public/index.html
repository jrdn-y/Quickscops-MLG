MLG Rules

  • Intervention only. No secondary, no equipment, no perks.
  • One shot, one kill — any hitbox.
  • Elimination: one life per round, first team to 6 rounds wins.
  • 90 s round timer. When it expires the team with more players alive takes the round; tie = draw.
  • Bolt cycle 1.3 s between shots. You can't scope while cycling.
  • Scope-in takes ~0.22 s. Firing before you're fully scoped spreads the shot — time your quickscope.
  • No-scopes, jumpshots, dropshots and longshots are all legal and get medals.
  • No friendly fire. Spectate teammates when you're down.

Settings & Controls

  • Keyboard/Mouse: WASD move · Mouse look · RMB scope · LMB fire · Shift sprint · Space jump · C crouch · Tab scoreboard · Esc pause
  • Controller (Xbox / PlayStation / most USB pads — just plug in and press a button): Left stick move · Right stick look · LT scope · RT fire · A jump · B or R3 crouch · L3 sprint · Y scoreboard · LB/RB spectate · Start pause. Menus: D-pad / stick + A.
