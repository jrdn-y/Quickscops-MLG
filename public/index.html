MLG Quickscope Rules

  • Snipers only — Intervention, Barrett .50cal, WA2000, M21 EBR. No secondaries, no grenades, no killstreaks.
  • One shot, one kill on any hitbox.
  • No hardscoping. Once you're fully scoped you have 1 second to shoot. Kills after that don't count.
  • Legal attachments: FMJ (wallbangs) and Extended Mags only.
  • Legal perks: Sleight of Hand, Marathon, Lightweight, Cold-Blooded, Steady Aim, Ninja. Commando and knife lunges are banned.
  • Throwing knives allowed — one per life.
  • Medals: Quickscope, No-Scope, 360, Jumpshot, Wallbang, Collateral, Longshot, Headshot, Bullseye.
  • Modes: Elimination (first to 6), Search & Destroy (first to 4), TDM, FFA.
  • No friendly fire. Fall off Skyline and it's a suicide.

Settings & Controls

  • Keyboard/Mouse: WASD move · Mouse look · RMB scope · LMB fire · Shift sprint (hold breath while scoped) · Space jump · C crouch · Z or Ctrl prone · R reload · F plant/defuse (hold) · G throwing knife · Tab scores · Esc pause
  • Controller (Xbox / PlayStation / most USB pads — plug in and press a button): Left stick move · Right stick look · LT scope · RT fire · A jump · B crouch (hold for prone) · X reload (hold to plant/defuse) · RB throwing knife · L3 sprint / hold breath · Back or Y scores · LB/RB spectate · Start pause. Menus: D-pad/stick + A, B to go back.
